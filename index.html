Frequently asked questions

Key information regarding our general merchandise capabilities, commercial consultancy, corporate registration, and contract execution.

What general merchandise does COLLOXS DERIVATIVES LTD supply?

We supply a diverse range of general commercial merchandise, including manufactured goods, office and operational equipment, consumer products, industrial consumables, packaging supplies, and raw materials. We source and deliver according to strict contract specifications and delivery timelines across Nigeria.

What commercial contracts and consultancy services do you provide?

Our consultancy practice provides end-to-end support for procurement strategy, contract structuring, vendor representation, commission agency, supply chain coordination, and commercial dispute mitigation. We assist corporate bodies, government suppliers, and private enterprises in navigating procurement and commercial engagements.

Is COLLOXS DERIVATIVES LTD fully registered with the CAC?

Yes. COLLOXS DERIVATIVES LTD is legally incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of the Federal Republic of Nigeria under RC Number 7279108 as a private company limited by shares, operating in compliance with the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA 2020).
